Hi, that's me!

I started out in media sales, later built sales teams and took on responsibility for marketing. Throughout my career, I kept seeing the same thing: great people spending their time on spreadsheets and handovers instead of conversations. Today, I work to eliminate exactly that. I’m hands-on in the system, not in theory. First understanding how you sell, then building pipelines, lifecycle stages, and automations that actually fit the way your business works.

Why work with me?

  • I bring a blend of creativity and strategy, ensuring innovative solutions tailored to your needs.
  • I know processes, challenges and KPIs in Sales, media and marketing
  • My strong organizational and analytical skills enable me to manage complex projects with ease.
  • I work cross-frunctional in German, English or Italian
  • I’m passionate about helping brands grow by translating their visions into actionable campaigns.
